live — wolt-watch.pages.dev

Wolt Watch

A live "can I order right now?" board for Tel Aviv Wolt venues. Wolt lists opening hours, but a kitchen can be within its hours and still not accepting orders. Wolt Watch shows the state that actually matters, and pings you the second a tracked place flips back online. Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS frontend with a Cloudflare edge-function proxy that works around Wolt's CORS blocks, plus an 8-second cache and retry to stay polite to the API.

  • vanilla-js
  • cloudflare-workers
  • edge-proxy
